## quiz

Exactly 5 multiple-choice questions.
- Ask ONLY about facts that the lecture states explicitly. If you cannot point to a sentence in the lecture, do not ask the question.
- `options`: exactly 4 plausible options; only one is correct.
- `answer_index`: the index (0-3) of the correct option.
- `explanation`: one or two sentences on why the correct option is right.
- `source_quote`: a short quote (max 25 words) copied from the lecture that proves the answer.
- `page`: where the answer appears.
Mix easy and harder questions. Do not make the correct option always the longest one.

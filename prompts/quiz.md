## quiz

About {{n_quiz}} multiple-choice questions.
- Ask ONLY about facts that the lecture states explicitly. If you cannot point to a sentence in the lecture, do not ask the question.
- Test understanding, not only recall: include questions that ask the student to apply a fact (e.g. which structure to choose, what an algorithm returns, which complexity holds in a given case).
- `options`: exactly 4 different options; only one is correct. Wrong options must be plausible mistakes a student could make (e.g. a related complexity, the other algorithm), never jokes or obviously wrong. Do not use "όλα τα παραπάνω" or "κανένα από τα παραπάνω".
- `answer_index`: the index (0-3) of the correct option.
- `explanation`: one or two sentences on why the correct option is right and, if useful, why the most tempting wrong option is wrong.
- `source_quote`: a short quote (max 25 words) copied word for word from the lecture text that proves the answer.
- `difficulty`: "easy", "medium" or "hard". Roughly a third of each.
- `page`: where the answer appears.
Keep all options of similar length and style.

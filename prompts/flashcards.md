## flashcards

About {{n_flashcards}} flashcards for spaced-repetition study (at least 2 for every page that teaches something).
- `front`: ONE precise question with a single clear answer. Not a bare term; ask e.g. "Ποια είναι η χρονική πολυπλοκότητα της BFS με λίστες γειτνίασης;", "Γιατί η DFS δεν εγγυάται συντομότερα μονοπάτια;", "Ποια δομή δεδομένων χρησιμοποιεί η BFS και γιατί;".
- `back`: a short, exact answer (max 2 sentences, ideally under 25 words). Start with the answer itself, then at most one clause of explanation.
- `kind`: one of
  - "definition": what a term means,
  - "concept": a property, rule or fact,
  - "process": the steps of an algorithm or procedure,
  - "comparison": the difference between two methods or structures,
  - "application": when or why to use something, or what happens on a given input,
  - "code": what a piece of code from the lecture does or computes.
- Mix the kinds: at most half the cards may be "definition". Include "comparison" and "application" cards whenever the lecture allows.
- One fact per card. No two cards about the same fact. No yes/no questions.
- `page`: where the answer appears.
